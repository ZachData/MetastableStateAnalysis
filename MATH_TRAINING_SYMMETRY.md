## 1. The question, and the lens

The theory this project tests proves clustering for **chosen** weights (identity-like, symmetric
QK, tied layers). Random-weight theory covers **step 0**. Pythia's trained weights are neither,
and nothing in the repo describes **the path** between them (`FUTURE_IDEAS.md` §0, "the
selection map").

The lens (geometric deep learning's, turned onto the parameters): a parameter space Θ, a group
G of transforms that leave the computed function unchanged, and the function living on Θ / G.
Three consequences frame every question below:

| # | Consequence | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | "The trained weights" are a G-orbit, not a point; only G-invariant quantities compare runs, seeds and the three reference points | random / identity / trained become three places in the quotient, each comparable by invariants |
| L2 | Each continuous symmetry gives a **conservation law** under gradient flow (Noether-type) | conserved quantities pin the flow to a leaf fixed by the init: an exact form of "the parameters shrink into a subset" |
| L3 | The **optimiser** need not respect G: weight decay, finite steps, Adam's coordinate-wise scaling break the laws, each in its own way | the drift of the conserved quantities is a fingerprint of how the optimiser, not the data, shapes the trained set |

## 3. What is already established (the starting point)

**Pythia's parameterisation** (410m; to re-check against `config.json` before relying on a
number): d = 1024, 16 heads, d_h = 64, rotary on 25 % of each head (16 dims, 8 frequency
pairs; 48 non-rotary), parallel attention and MLP, LayerNorm with gain and bias, GeLU MLP,
untied embeddings, QKV and output biases. AdamW β (0.9, 0.95), **weight decay 0.01 on matrices,
not on gains or biases**, clip 1.0 (`trackT_timaeus/design-T5.md` "optimizer"). Init:
`small_init` σ = √(2/5d) on embeddings, QKV, MLP-in; `wang_init` σ = 2/(L√d) on attention-out
and MLP-out (`p1d_cluster_ensemble/lit-1d.md` row 2).

**Checked closed forms** (`tools/math_checks/ts_conservation.py`, which lists what each does not prove):

| # | Statement |
|---|---|
| F1 | If the loss sees (W_Q, W_K) only through M = W_Qᵀ W_K, gradient flow conserves Ψ_QK = W_Q W_Qᵀ − W_K W_Kᵀ (d_h × d_h). Same for OV: Ψ_OV = W_V W_Vᵀ − W_Oᵀ W_O |
| F2 | Weight decay λ on both factors: Ψ(t) = e^{−2λt} Ψ(0) |
| F3 | A LayerNorm gain γ_i feeding column i of W, decay on W only: d/dt(γ_i² − ‖W[:, i]‖²) = 2λ‖W[:, i]‖² |
| F4 | RoPE: the QK symmetry per rotary pair shrinks from GL(2) to {aI + bJ} (a complex scalar) |
| F5 | Random init: the QK symmetric energy fraction has ratio-of-expectations (1 + 1/d)/2 = **0.500488** at d = 1024 |

**F5 against the repo's data.** `PROJECT.md` §3.12 block J (`tools/run/qk_symmetry_sweep.py`,
410m, non-rotary 48 dims) reads the median symmetric fraction at **0.5005 at step 512** and
**0.5231 at 143000**; identity weights give 1.0. So at 512 the QK products still sit at the
random value of this invariant, and by the end they have moved about 2 % of the way to
identity on it. The spread of the random value (needed to say how many standard deviations
0.523 is) is not computed.

**A consequence of the init, from F1 (derived, not measured).** `wang_init` makes W_O far
smaller than W_V at step 0, so Ψ_OV(0) ≈ d(σ_small² − σ_wang²) I_{d_h}, positive definite.
Under gradient flow with decay that imbalance only shrinks as e^{−2λt}; under exact
conservation every head's OV would stay "V-heavy" for the whole run. How much Adam erases it is
an open measurement (§5 E1).

Other repo results the questions lean on: the heads' subspaces concentrate on shared residual
directions from 256–512 (`docs/ROADMAP.md` §1; Track S, `trackS_subspace/status-S.md`);
A1/A2's energy break at 512 and induction at 1000 (`trackA_force/`); Track T's LLC reads
(`trackT_timaeus/status-T.md`); 1d's three rogue coordinates (`p1d_cluster_ensemble/status-1d.md`).

## 6. Telling tractable from intractable before investing

1. **Does a small set of variables close on itself?** (M with balanced init does; one head inside a trained network does not.) If predicting one parameter needs all of them, stop.
2. **Is there a limit where it closes** (many heads, small η, small init), and is Pythia near it?
3. **Does a solvable toy reproduce the phenomenon?** If it vanishes in every solvable toy, the toy's theory will not transfer.
4. **Can it be checked numerically in an afternoon** before it is proved? Q1–Q6 can.
