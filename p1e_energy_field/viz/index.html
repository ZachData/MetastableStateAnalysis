Does the residual stream climb its own energy field?

Phase 1e reads pythia-410m's residual stream as the field of the particle picture, φβ(x) = Σj exp(β⟨x, uj⟩) on unit LN1 rows, at the β the model was measured to have, instead of as clusters. It asks where the field's wells are (U1), and whether each token's update moves up the field (attraction) or down it (repulsion) (U2).

U2 · the move against the field

It depends on the training stage

Up to step 1000, most of the climb is a shift every token shares, supplied by the MLP. Attention's part specific to each token goes down the field, and against each head's own kernel, head by head.

From step 2000, attention's mean pull in the last layers supplies the shared climb. From 4000, attention summed over heads climbs its heads' kernels while more single heads still go down their own kernel than up it.

U1 · the field at the tokens

One well

At β 3.5, every trained layer band at every step has a single basin holding every token, except L1–8 at step 128. Across 1.6–5.6 the only exceptions are at step 128. A few wells appear near β 10 and many by β 20.

The field's density is lumpier than a matched Gaussian cloud's, already at step 0. It is mostly each token's closeness to the cloud's mean: punctuation at the centre, content words at the edge.

At the measured β the field is one basin

Wells are found by mean shift on φβ from every token; a token's well is its basin. keff = exp(H) of the basin shares counts wells weighted by the tokens they hold, so 1 means a single basin. The sweep runs at layers 4, 12 and 20, with the median over the 8 passages. The shaded band is β's measured interval.

Over all 432 sweep cells (3 layers × 18 steps × 8 passages) at β 10, 196 have 2–20 wells, 111 have one and 125 more than 20. At β 20, 382 have more than 20. The merge tolerance does not change the count: at 1e-4 and 1e-2, 0 of 10,368 long-passage cells change.

What is still open

  • One well, as the theory expects? Whether a single basin at β 3.5 is what the theory predicts for n ≈ 2,000 tokens in d = 1,024 is unread. A reading of the single-cluster thresholds in 2312.10794 would make this result the theory's expectation rather than a null.
  • The late split between heads and their sum. It could be heads moving tokens towards where other heads attend, or a few large heads. The saved means cannot split them.
  • The two wells in L1–8 at step 128, the one trained exception. They sit inside U2's 32–256 window and are free to read from the saved labels.
  • Wells at β ≈ 10, the one range with a few, are parked and labelled off the measured β (1.8× its interval's top).
  • U4 (repulsive regions) stays fenced until P-S1 is scored: its mean pairwise inner product is P-S1's degree-1 moment. U5 (correlated against anti-correlated token pairs) needs a corpus for co-occurrence counts.
